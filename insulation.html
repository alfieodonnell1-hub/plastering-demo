Kingspan and Rockwool insulation in Cheshire

Thermal and acoustic insulation for walls, ceilings and floors, using Kingspan rigid boards and Rockwool stone wool.

What it is

Warmer, quieter rooms

Kingspan makes rigid insulation boards that give a high level of thermal insulation in a thin layer, including insulated plasterboard that boards and insulates a wall in one go.

Rockwool is stone wool insulation. It fits between studs, joists and rafters, improves sound insulation and is non-combustible, which makes it a common choice for partitions and between floors. Many jobs use both.

FAQs

Insulation questions, answered

Kingspan or Rockwool: which do I need?

They do different jobs. Kingspan boards give the most thermal insulation for their thickness, which saves room space. Rockwool is better for sound and is non-combustible. The right choice depends on where it’s going.

Can you insulate walls from the inside?

Yes. Cold or solid external walls can be lined with insulated plasterboard, or with a stud wall filled with insulation, then plastered. It takes a little floor space, and the build-up needs to control condensation.
