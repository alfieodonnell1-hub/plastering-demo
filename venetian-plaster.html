What it is

Polished by hand, layer by layer

Venetian plaster is a traditional Italian finish made from lime and marble dust. It is applied in thin layers with a steel trowel and burnished to a polished sheen, so light moves across the wall and gives it real depth.

It is used on feature walls, hallways, living rooms and bathrooms at home, and in restaurants, hotels and shops. It can be tinted to almost any colour.

FAQs

Venetian plaster questions, answered

How is Venetian plaster different from paint?

It is built up by hand in thin layers and then polished, so it has a depth and sheen paint can’t give. Every wall is slightly different.

Can Venetian plaster go in a bathroom?

Yes, with a protective wax or sealer. Inside the shower itself, microcement or another specialist finish is usually a better fit.
