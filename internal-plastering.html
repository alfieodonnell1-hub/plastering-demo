What it is

Flat, smooth and ready to decorate

Internal plastering covers skimming, where a thin finish coat goes over existing plaster or plasterboard, and full re-plastering, where old or damaged plaster is taken back and replaced.

It is part of most renovations, extensions and rewires, and the quality of the plastering decides how good the walls look once they are painted.

FAQs

Internal plastering questions, answered

Do I need a skim or a full re-plaster?

If the existing plaster is sound, a skim over the top is usually enough. If it is loose, blown or badly damaged, it needs taking back and re-plastering. We check at survey.

How long before I can paint new plaster?

New plaster needs to dry fully first, which can take from a few days to a few weeks depending on thickness, heating and ventilation. It turns an even pale colour when dry. The first coat should be a mist coat of watered-down emulsion.
