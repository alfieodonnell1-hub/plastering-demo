Monocouche render in Cheshire

A through-coloured render applied as a single coat, for a clean, low-maintenance finish. We install all monocouche systems.

What it is

One coat, coloured all the way through

Monocouche is French for “single layer”. It is a cement-based render with the colour mixed in, applied as one thick coat in two passes and then scraped back to give an even, lightly textured finish.

Because the colour is in the render, it doesn’t need painting. It is mostly used on blockwork, which makes it a popular choice for new builds, extensions and modern re-renders.

Why choose it

Why clients choose Monocouche render

01

No painting

The colour runs through the render, so there is no paint to flake or redo.

02

Faster to apply

One coat instead of two or three means less time on site than traditional render.

03

Weather-resistant

It keeps rain out while still letting the wall breathe.

04

Consistent colour

Factory-mixed colours give an even finish across the whole building.

FAQs

Monocouche render questions, answered

What does monocouche mean?

It’s French for “single layer”. The render goes on as one coat, in two passes, and is scraped back once it has firmed up to give the finished texture.

What can monocouche go on?

It is designed mainly for blockwork and suitable masonry. Some existing walls need preparing or a base coat first, which we check at survey.
