What it is

The classic render, built up in coats

Sand and cement render is applied in two or three coats: a scratch coat that bonds to the wall, then a float coat that is trued up and finished. The finish can be smooth or textured.

It is a durable, cost-effective choice for modern brick and block walls, garden walls and outbuildings, and is usually finished with a masonry paint.

FAQs

Sand and cement render questions, answered

Does sand and cement render need painting?

Usually, yes. A masonry paint finishes the surface and adds weather protection. If you’d rather not paint, a through-coloured render such as K Rend or monocouche may suit better.

Is it suitable for older houses?

It suits most modern brick and blockwork. Older solid-walled buildings are often better with a breathable lime render, and we’ll advise at survey.
