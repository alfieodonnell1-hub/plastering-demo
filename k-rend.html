What it is

Colour that runs right through the render

K Rend is a range of factory-made renders. The colour is mixed into the material itself, so the finish you see goes the full depth of the top coat instead of sitting on the surface like paint.

It is used on house fronts and extensions, on full re-renders of older properties, and on commercial elevations. The range covers silicone thin coat and scraped textures, which is why we fit every system and help you choose the one that suits the wall.

Why choose it

Four reasons clients pick K Rend

01

No painting

The colour is in the render, so there is no paint film to peel or redo every few years.

02

Sheds water

Silicone in the finish helps rain run off the face of the wall instead of soaking in.

03

Lets the wall breathe

Moisture inside the wall can still escape through the finish.

04

A choice of looks

Different textures and a wide colour range, matched to the building at survey.

FAQs

K Rend questions, answered

Does K Rend need painting?

No. The colour runs through the finish, so it does not need a paint coat. An occasional wash keeps it looking fresh.

Can it go over my existing render or brickwork?

Often, yes, but it depends on the condition of what is there. We check the wall at the survey and tell you whether it can be rendered over or needs taking back first.
