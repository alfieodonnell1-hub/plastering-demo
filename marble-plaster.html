What it is

The look of stone, without the slabs

Marble plaster is made from lime and crushed marble. Depending on how it is applied and finished, it ranges from a soft, stone-like matte texture to a smooth sheen, and it can be veined to look like marble.

It brings the character of natural stone to feature walls, fireplaces, columns and curved surfaces, without the weight or joints of stone slabs. [CONFIRM THE MARBLE PLASTER FINISHES YOU OFFER]

FAQs

Marble plaster questions, answered

What’s the difference between Venetian and marble plaster?

Both are lime-based and applied by hand. Venetian plaster is polished to a high sheen, while marble plaster usually has more texture and a stone-like feel. Seeing samples side by side is the easiest way to choose.

Where can marble plaster be used?

Most internal walls, fireplaces, columns and curved surfaces. Areas that get wet need a suitable sealer.
