What it is

One continuous surface

Microcement is a thin cement-based coating, applied by hand in layers just a few millimetres thick and then sealed. It gives a smooth, seamless surface with no joints or grout.

Because it is so thin, it can often go straight over existing tiles, screed or plaster. It is used on floors, walls, showers, wet rooms, stairs and worktops, and often around swimming pools.

FAQs

Microcement questions, answered

Can microcement go over tiles?

Often, yes, if the tiles are firmly fixed. The grout lines are filled and a primer and mesh go down first, so the old surface doesn’t need ripping out.

Is microcement waterproof?

With the right sealer it is water-resistant, which is why it is used in showers, wet rooms and around pools. The sealer needs maintaining over time.
