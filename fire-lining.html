What it is

Boarding that holds back fire

Fire lining uses fire-rated plasterboard, and where needed stone wool insulation, to give walls, ceilings and steel beams a set period of fire resistance, usually 30 or 60 minutes or more.

It is needed in places like the wall between a house and an integral garage, stairways in loft conversions, flats, commercial units and around steelwork. A fire rating only holds if the whole system is installed as the manufacturer tested it.

FAQs

Fire lining questions, answered

Where is fire lining needed?

Common places are between a house and an integral garage, around stairways in loft conversions, between flats and around steel beams. Your designer or Building Control will say what’s needed.

What fire rating do I need?

Ratings are given in minutes, such as 30 or 60, and depend on the building and where the wall or ceiling is. The rating comes from your drawings or Building Control, and the system is built to meet it.
