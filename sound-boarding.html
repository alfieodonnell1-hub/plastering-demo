Sound boarding in Cheshire

Acoustic boards and insulation that cut noise through walls and ceilings, for homes, flats and offices.

Get a free quoteFree quote Call [PHONE NUMBER]
What it is

Keep noise where it belongs

Sound boarding uses dense acoustic plasterboard, acoustic insulation and, where needed, resilient bars that separate the boards from the wall or ceiling behind, so less sound passes through.

It is used between bedrooms, home offices and living spaces, on party walls, and in flats and conversions that need to meet sound insulation rules. It can be built into new stud walls or added to existing ones.

FAQs

Sound boarding questions, answered

How much quieter will it make a room?

It depends on the existing wall and the type of noise. Sound also travels through gaps, sockets, floors and ceilings, so the best results come from treating those routes too.

Can you soundproof an existing wall?

Yes. Common options are acoustic boards fixed on resilient bars, or a separate stud wall built in front and filled with acoustic insulation. Both take a little room space.
